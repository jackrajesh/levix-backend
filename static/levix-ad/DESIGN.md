# LEVIX 40s Ad Visual Identity

## Style Prompt
Premium SaaS product film for a modern business operating system. The feeling is calm control after operational noise: dark cinematic product environment, crisp white interface surfaces, saturated LEVIX blue accents, restrained green success states, and soft edge lighting. Motion should feel like Stripe, Linear, Notion, Shopify, and Apple product reveals: precise, quiet, dimensional, and practical.

## Colors
- Ink canvas: `#050914`
- Slate panel: `#0f172a`
- LEVIX blue: `#2563eb`
- Electric blue highlight: `#60a5fa`
- Success green: `#10b981`
- Warning amber: `#f59e0b`
- Paper white: `#f8fafc`
- Border mist: `#dbeafe`

## Typography
- Primary: `Plus Jakarta Sans`
- Fallback: `Inter`, `system-ui`, `sans-serif`
- Use tight but readable SaaS display type. No negative letter spacing beyond existing logo treatment.

## Motion Rules
- Use slow dolly pushes, macro UI focus, layered parallax, and smooth panel transitions.
- Chaos moves in short bursts; LEVIX moves in clean arcs and steady depth.
- Text enters with subtle y movement and opacity, never bouncy.
- Highlight real workflow causality: order received, stock updates, WhatsApp thread syncs, report generates.

## What Not To Do
- No meme cuts, glitch overload, crypto styling, fake testimonials, or exaggerated AI claims.
- No loud influencer pacing or cartoonish icons.
- No decorative orb backgrounds.
- Do not make the dashboard feel fictional; keep the workflow close to actual LEVIX website and dashboard surfaces.
