# LEVIX 40s Premium Ad Sequence

## Core Positioning
LEVIX turns everyday shop operations from scattered messages, paper, memory, and spreadsheets into one calm business operating system: orders, inventory, WhatsApp, customers, reports, analytics, and teams.

## Voiceover Script
0.0-5.0: "Running a business should not feel like chasing messages, stock, and memory."

5.0-12.0: "Meet LEVIX. A smarter way to manage your shop from one organized system."

12.0-26.0: "Orders arrive, stock updates, customers stay connected, and your reports become clear enough to act on."

26.0-34.0: "Less stress. More control. Built for businesses that want to grow with confidence."

34.0-40.0: "LEVIX. Modern business management. Start simplifying your business."

## Camera And Motion
- 0-5s: handheld-feeling micro-shakes only on chaos cards, 20px max movement, fast notification streaks.
- 5-12s: hard silence, then a 3D dolly into LEVIX with a clean blue edge light.
- 12-26s: macro product passes. Each workflow panel moves on a different depth plane: sidebar slow, content medium, highlights fastest.
- 26-34s: slow lateral drift, softened contrast, relaxed pacing.
- 34-40s: centered lockup, slight push-in, premium fade.

## UI Sections Used
- Captured homepage hero: promise, CTA, trust markers.
- Captured feature grid: orders, inventory, WhatsApp, customers, sales reports, team access.
- Captured pricing/auth surfaces: used as credibility and conversion layers.
- Reconstructed dashboard surfaces: dark sidebar, inbox, orders, inventory, analytics cards, and reports based on `templates/dashboard.html`.

## Soundtrack Timing
- 0-5s: sparse low note, muted room texture, notification tension.
- 5-12s: pulse starts at 92 BPM equivalent, calm bass, brushed transient.
- 12-26s: minimal tech rhythm, no EDM drop, synced UI ticks on feature changes.
- 26-34s: harmonic lift, warmer pad, rhythm thins.
- 34-40s: final chord and short clean tail.

## Render Intent
Duration: 40 seconds. Aspect: 1920x1080. Target: 30fps or 60fps. Tone: funded SaaS startup launch commercial, premium but grounded.
