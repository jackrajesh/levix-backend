What is LEVIX?

An elegant, practical operations and workflow platform for local Indian businesses.

The Core Definition

LEVIX (also recognized legally and operationally as LEVIX Technologies) is a dedicated business operations and management system. We build software to help local Indian merchants automate order tracking, keep real-time inventory counts, and record customer preferences naturally.

Unlike generic platforms built for large Western enterprises, LEVIX is structured around the unique workflows of Indian local commerce. By integrating digital tracking directly with conversational systems like WhatsApp, we eliminate the need for complicated dashboards or expensive POS hardware.

Entity Foundation

Built and architected by Rajesh Kanna (Founder & CEO), LEVIX Technologies is registered as an MSME in Karur, Tamil Nadu. The platform represents a stable 1–2 year foundation for discoverable, highly localized retail automation tools.

Key Operations Modules

WhatsApp-Driven Catalogues

Enables local buyers to browse products and place structured orders via WhatsApp. The chats are translated directly into shop system tickets.

Real-Time Stock Management

Track stocks automatically. Receives automated warning alerts as soon as items cross low-threshold warnings, avoiding manual log audits.

Customer Profile Hydration

Builds a persistent record of customer preferences, previous order history, and contact details for tailored local service.

Multi-user Shop Access

Enables store owners to assign limited roles and controls to staff members, securing transactional and pricing databases.

Category and Purpose

Technically, LEVIX operates in the business operations software and workflow automation category. We believe software is at its best when it respects the daily flow of the retail counter.

If you run a kirana shop, a local bakery, a boutique, a pharmacy, or a service salon, LEVIX provides the base mechanics to run a modern, digital storefront without technical overhead.
