About LEVIX

Shop management built in India for Indian local businesses.

Why We Built LEVIX

Running a local shop in India comes with a lot of daily challenges — managing orders over phone and WhatsApp, keeping track of stock manually, remembering customer preferences, and handling billing without a proper system.

Most existing software is too expensive, too complicated, or built for large enterprises. LEVIX is a simple, affordable shop operations platform designed specifically for the way Indian local businesses actually work.

What Levix Does

Levix gives you one dashboard to manage your entire shop:

Order Management — Receive and track customer orders. Whether orders come via WhatsApp, phone, or walk-in, everything is in one place.

Inventory Tracking — Know exactly what is in stock, what is running low, and what needs reordering. No more guessing or manual registers.

Customer Records — Build a digital record of your customers automatically. Track order history and contact details for better service.

WhatsApp Integration — Let customers browse your catalogue and place orders via WhatsApp — the app they already use every day.

Sales Logs & Reports — Understand daily sales, best-selling products, and basic trends with clear, actionable summaries.
