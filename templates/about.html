About LEVIX Technologies

Practical billing, inventory, POS-style order management, and WhatsApp workflow software for Indian MSMEs.

Our Origin Story

Operating a physical business in India is a live balancing act. Merchants must manage incoming walk-in buyers, handle stream of catalog enquiries on WhatsApp, check physical stock availability, and record local credit history manually.

LEVIX Technologies was founded by Rajesh Kanna to provide a dedicated, lightweight operations engine for domestic MSMEs. We build software tools that fit standard retail flows natively, avoiding the bloat and complexity of general enterprise systems.

Core Operational Values

🎯 Direct Utility

Shop operations should not require tutorials. Every screen is designed to be understood and used by any team member in less than two minutes.

🇮🇳 Domestic Alignment

From local Kiranas and sweet bakeries to boutiques and salons, we align our system directly with Indian retail structures.

💰 Highly Accessible

High-quality workflow automation should be affordable. We offer a transparent SaaS platform starting at ₹799/month.

⚡ Structured Isolation

Your sales records, customer lists, and stock balances are strictly isolated, securely stored, and highly available.
